# Lot 6.9 - Shooting feel (internal lot)

Not in the reference plan. Opened after the first playtests: shooting feels dead (no flash, no sound, no impact feedback) and the first-person weapon is drawn as a normal world object that clips through walls.

- **Model/effort:** Sonnet, high | **Depends on:** 6.6, 6.7 | **Branch:** `lot/6.9-shooting-feel` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `client` (weapon view, effects, audio wiring). **Additive changes allowed in:** `render`, `shaders`, `assets` (sound/effect data), `docs`.

## Goal

Every shot is readable and satisfying: you see it, hear it and see what it hit.

## Spec

1. **Muzzle flash** (short additive sprite and light) and **tracers** (fast streaks from the muzzle to the hit point).
2. **Weapon feel:** visible recoil kick and recovery, walk/idle sway and bob, small camera shake on shot and on explosion; tuned per weapon from existing weapon data (no new gameplay values).
3. **Material-based impacts:** sparks on metal/concrete, dust on stone/sand, wood chips on wood, splash on water, snow puff; short-lived bullet decals on surfaces (capped ring buffer). Decision: how the hit surface material is looked up.
4. **Hit marker** and a distinct kill marker on the HUD; hit direction indicator when damaged.
5. **Sound:** the game has no in-game audio yet. Add shot, reload, impact (per material), footstep, hit/kill sounds, using the existing audio settings (volume). Decision: sounds generated in code (simple synthesis, no binary assets) and mixed through the audio backend of the settings lot; if none exists, pick the smallest maintained crate and record it.
6. **FPS weapon fix:** the held weapon is drawn in a separate pass/depth range (own projection, depth cleared or squeezed) so it never clips through walls, and is not lit/shadowed like a world prop.

## Acceptance

`cargo xtask ci` green in Docker; lavapipe render test of the weapon view against a wall (no clipping); unit tests for recoil curves and material lookup; effect pools are bounded. Out of scope: terrain, props, maps.
