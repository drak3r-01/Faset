# Lot 8.6 - Prop library, outdoor themes (phase 8, world)

Not in the reference plan. Phase 8 turns the arena maps into believable worlds (see `README.md`).

- **Model/effort:** Sonnet, medium | **Depends on:** 8.5 | **Branch:** `lot/8.6-prop-content-outdoor` (one PR)
- **Owns:** Data only, `assets/props/<theme>/*.ron`. No Rust code; if a recipe needs a missing operation, stop and report.

## Goal

About 100 new or reworked props with real charm for nature, coast, desert, snow, quarry and rural themes.

## Spec

1. Rocks and boulders in several shapes, cliffs pieces, logs, stumps, fences, stone walls, hay bales, carts, boats, docks, barrels, buoys, huts, tents, campfires, signs, benches, lamp posts.
2. Three to five variants per prop through seeds and wear; stylised clean look, consistent proportions (a human is 1.8 m).
3. Collision declared on every recipe; hero props get LOD.
4. One theme per session, validate and review the contact sheet after each.

## Acceptance

`asset-tool validate assets/props` zero errors; budgets respected; contact sheets reviewed visually (result in the report); at least 100 recipes added or reworked.
