# Lot 4.10 — Bots: decision, perception, aim, difficulty levels
- Model/effort: Sonnet, high | Depends on: 4.9, 4.7 | Milestone: J4 (an expert bot beats an easy bot over 20 matches)

## Goal
Make bots play: utility-based decisions, delayed perception, aim with ballistic lead and realistic error, four difficulty levels, and a per-tick budget suitable for 16 bots.

## Crates
- Owned: `bots` (decision, perception, aim, levels, budget, bot_fill rule).
- Read-only: `sim` (physics, controller, line-of-sight raycast), `minigame-api` (match state, services, mode commands), `bots` navigation from lot 4.9 (same crate), `core`.
- Forbidden: public interfaces of `sim`, `minigame-api`, `server`, `net`, `protocol`, `core`, `mode-fps`. The `server` loop must treat bots as virtual clients through the existing input path; if that needs a server change, stop and report.
- Weapon parameters (projectile speed, gravity factor, range) reach bots as data passed in (bots depends on sim and minigame-api only), not by depending on mode-fps.

## Spec
### Decision (utility AI)
Each tick every bot scores its possible actions; the best-scoring one wins. The current action gets a 15 % bonus to avoid hesitation.
| Action | Score rises when |
|---|---|
| Attack | an enemy is visible and within weapon range |
| Pursue | an enemy was seen less than 5 s ago |
| Heal | health is low and a heal is near |
| Pick up | a useful bonus is near |
| Objective | the mode designates one |
| Patrol | nothing else applies |
Heal example: U_heal = (1 - PV/PV_max) * w / (1 + d/k), with d the distance to the heal and w, k tuning constants (not given in the plan: set in config, report).

### Perception
Field of view 110 degrees, limited range (configured), line of sight verified by a raycast. Observations pass through a queue delayed by the difficulty's reaction time. Shots close to the bot are heard (position known, no visibility).

### Aim
Ballistic lead: for projectile speed s, relative target position D, target velocity v_c, the aim time t is the smallest positive root of
(v_c.v_c - s^2) t^2 + 2 (D.v_c) t + D.D = 0.
Gravity: raise the aim by 1/2 g t^2, computed in two iterations.
Lob (high arc) for horizontal distance x and height difference y:
theta = atan((s^2 +/- sqrt(s^4 - g (g x^2 + 2 y s^2))) / (g x)). Use the + root for the lob, the - root for the direct arc (interpretation; confirm and report).
Tracking: an angular spring towards the target with capped rotation speed.
Error: time-correlated noise, e <- e exp(-dt/tau) + sigma sqrt(1 - exp(-2 dt/tau)) N(0,1); tau and sigma depend on the level. Gives believable tremble, not white noise.

### Difficulty levels
| Level | Reaction | Aim error | Movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 600 ms | strong | simple running |
| Normal | 400 ms | medium | lateral dodge |
| Hard | 250 ms | weak | dodge and slides |
| Expert | 150 ms | very weak | bunny hop on straight lines, slide-hop |
Movement capabilities are flags in the level (bunny hop, slide, slide-hop, dodge) used by the path follower of lot 4.9.

### Budget and fill
Target: 16 bots in under 1 ms per tick. Line-of-sight tests are spread: each bot performs one test every 4 ticks.
Setting `bot_fill` keeps the total player count at a target: `bots_wanted = max(0, bot_fill - humans)`. A bot yields its slot when a human joins.

## Public interfaces to create or respect
- `BotBrain::new(level: Level, seed: u64)` and `BotBrain::think(&mut self, view: &BotView, dt: f32) -> InputCommand` (same input type as humans, lot 1.x `protocol` quantised commands).
- `Level` (serde, TOML/RON table with the four levels), `BotView` (perceived state built from delayed observations), `plan_bot_fill(humans, bot_fill) -> usize`.
- Deterministic randomness from a seed given at construction.

## Acceptance criteria
- `cargo xtask test`:
  - `expert_beats_easy_20_matches`: 20 seeded 1v1 matches on the sample map (fixed seeds, fixed map); the Expert bot wins strictly more matches than the Easy bot. Record the score in the report.
  - Aim: lead time matches the root formula within 1 %; gravity compensation converges in two iterations.
  - Perception: a target behind a wall is not seen; a target at 111 degrees is not seen; the observation appears only after the reaction delay.
  - `bot_fill`: with 3 humans and bot_fill = 8 the bots count is 5; a human joining removes one bot.
  - Budget: a benchmark with 16 bots reports the mean tick cost; it must be under 1 ms on the reference host (report the number).
- `cargo xtask ci` is green.
