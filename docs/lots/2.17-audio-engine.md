# Lot 2.17 — `audio`: engine, synthesised effects, 3D spatialisation
- Model/effort: Sonnet, medium | Depends on: 0.2 | Milestone: none

## Goal
Create the `audio` crate: a `kira`-based engine with 32 prioritised voices, effects synthesised from RON recipes, and 3D positioning with distance attenuation, panning, occlusion and underwater filtering.

## Crates
- Owns: `audio`. Depends on `core` only. Must not depend on `render`, `sim` or `dedicated`.
- `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` (`unsafe` is allowed only in `render` and `upscale`).
- Errors: `thiserror`. Logs: `tracing`. Units: metres, seconds. Y up, -Z forward.
- Forbidden: changing the public interface of any other crate.

## Spec
- Engine: `kira`. Audio runs on the thread managed by `kira`; no extra thread of our own.
- Separate buses: general, effects, interface. A music bus is reserved (music is an open point of the plan).
- 32 simultaneous voices with priorities. When the limit is reached, the lowest-priority voice is stopped; among equal priorities, the oldest is stopped (report this rule).
- Effects are synthesised at load from RON recipes: waveform, envelope, pitch glide, noise. No audio file is used.
- Spatialisation:
  - Distance attenuation (plan section 19, verbatim). `k` and `d_ref` have no values in the plan: choose them, and report the values chosen.
```
G(d) = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(\frac{d_{ref}}{d_{ref} + k\,(d - d_{ref})},\ 0,\ 1\right)
```
  - Panning along the listener's right axis. With -Z forward and Y up, right = forward x up (equals +X in the identity pose). Pan sign follows the dot product with right.
  - Low-pass filter when the source is hidden by a wall or the listener is underwater.
- Volume settings exposed through the API: general, effects, interface, output device, key sound (the settings screen is lot 6.4).
- Tests must run without a sound device (Docker has none): use a null or offline backend.

## Public interfaces to create or respect
- `audio::Engine::new(config)`, `Engine::play(recipe_id, Position3D, priority)`, `Engine::set_listener(position, forward, up)`, `Engine::set_bus_volume(bus, value)`, `audio::Recipe` (RON), `audio::synthesise(recipe, sample_rate) -> Vec<f32>`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Unit test: a source at the listener's right gives a right pan above 0.5; a source at the left gives a left pan.
- Unit test: attenuation is monotonic with distance.
- Unit test: playing 40 sounds leaves exactly 32 voices active and keeps the highest-priority ones.
- Unit test: synthesis of a recipe gives a buffer of length duration x sample rate, deterministic for the same input.
- Unit test: a low-pass is applied when the source is occluded or the listener is underwater.
- `cargo tree -p dedicated` does not contain `audio`.
- `cargo xtask ci` passes in Docker.

## Out of scope
- Music and audio files.
- Device selection UI (lot 6.4).
- Sound on the server.
