# Lot 6.8 - Map redesign (internal lot)

Not in the reference plan. Opened with 6.7 after a first test of the Windows build: the twelve maps of lot 5.6 were walls and plain boxes with white borders. This lot replaces them with four designed maps, each with a theme, a believable setting and natural borders, and makes the client use the theme of the map it plays.

- **Model/effort:** Opus, high | **Depends on:** 6.6, 6.7, 3.x, 4.9, 5.5 | **Branch:** `claude/project-thread-fv86de` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `assets/maps`, `tools/map-scripts`, `client::look`. **Additive changes allowed in:** `client` (executable wiring), `docs`.

## Goal

Four team maps (2 to 14 players) that look like places, with routes, cover, height and sightlines worth playing, no flat white wall around them, and a renderer that shows each one with its own light, sky, weather, water and lamps.

## The maps

All four are Rotate180-symmetric (a fair team layout: both teams have the same walking distances to every major pickup) and validate with `map-tool validate` (0 errors, 0 warnings).

| Map | Setting | Natural border | Routes and height |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Driftwood Cove** (`day`, clear) | tropical island in a turquoise lagoon: beaches, palms, a grass plateau, a 3-tier ziggurat with a shrine, piers with fishing huts, a shipwreck, two watchtowers | the sea is deadly (a KillVolume under the water level, the renderer draws the sea), a ragged ring of basalt cliffs and sea stacks | beach ring (long sightlines), plateau with ruined walls and crates, ziggurat (shrine at 5 m), 4 m watchtowers; spawn beaches are sheltered by rock walls |
| **Red Mesa** (`sunset`, dusk) | dry canyon: two mesas, a river bed with ramps and two wooden bridges, mushroom rocks, wagon wreck, water tower, cacti, a lookout ledge | terraced sandstone and clay canyon walls up to 18 m | mesas at 3 m reached by stairs and a rock gate, river bed at -1.5 m, cap rocks at 4 m, water tower deck at 5 m |
| **Pine Hollow** (`snow`, overcast, snowfall) | alpine valley: frozen lake, two wooden cabins with a rooftop ramp, a ridge with a watch hut, pines, snowmen, wood stacks | mountains of stone and ice with snow caps up to 24 m | cabin roofs (4 m), ridge (2 m, hut at 5 m), lake as an open centre |
| **Rust Harbor** (`night`, stars) | container quay between two dark basins: two moored cargo ships with gangways, a gantry crane, container lanes, fuel tank, warehouses with roof terraces, sodium lamps | deadly water on both long sides, breakwater rocks, brick warehouses at both ends | ship decks (1.5 m, bridge house to 6 m), container stacks with ramps (up to 7.8 m), warehouse roofs (6.5 m) |

The maps are authored by `tools/map-scripts/build.py` (run in the dev container: `docker compose run --rm dev python3 tools/map-scripts/build.py`); the output is plain RON that the editor opens like any other.

## What was done

1. **Maps:** the four files above in `assets/maps`. The twelve maps of lot 5.6 (including Arena Zero) are removed; the rotation and the menu catalogue read `assets/maps`, so they now offer the four. `assets/maps/example.ron` (the fixture of many tests and the map embedded in the executable) moved to `assets/testdata/example.ron`.
2. **Natural borders:** the sea (a KillVolume below the water level, so touching the water means drowning and respawning), cliff and mountain rings, sea stacks, breakwater rocks, buildings. No white wall.
3. **Map look in the client** (`client::look::MapLook`): the map's theme drives the frame: ambience profile (falling back to `day`), sky hour and cloud cover, the weather calendar (stepwise), the point lights of the file (up to `MAX_POINT_LIGHTS`) and the water (sea level = highest top of the map's `Water` zones, gentle Gerstner waves, underwater effects when the camera is below). Previously the executable drew every map with `day`.
4. **Lamp materials** (`lamp_warm`, `lamp_white`, `lamp_red`, `lamp_green`, ids 50 to 53): steady emissive colours for the lamps of a night map.
5. **Tests:** `look` unit tests (every map names a real profile, the harbor is a night scene with lamps and a sea, the hollow snows, the calendar order, name fallbacks), `map_looks` on lavapipe (each map drawn from a spawn and from above with its look, no validation message; `FASET_MAP_SHOTS=<dir>` keeps the images).

|---|---|---|---|
| Driftwood Cove | 187 | 9.1 m | 46 % (seed 1), 23 % (seed 2) |
| Red Mesa | 178 | 11.3 m | 0 % |
| Pine Hollow | 275 | 15.1 m | 1 % |
| Rust Harbor | 230 | 9.4 m | 0 % |

Cove is the open one: the sheltered spawn beaches reduced but did not remove spawn deaths (the island is small and bots go straight for the enemy spawn). Tuning it further (more cover lines on the beaches, a second route) is open.

## Decisions

- **Sea:** water is deadly, not swimmable (the simulation has no swimming; `Water` zones are events only). The renderer draws the water plane at the zone's top.
- **Decor from brushes:** palms, pines, rocks, crates and ships are boxes, wedges and prisms, so decor is solid and costs no new mesh path (the client does not render the `props` of a map yet).
- **Symmetry:** the whole layout is Rotate180 so rule 9 holds exactly; spawn teams swap under the half turn.
- **Players:** 2 to 12 on the cove, 2 to 14 on the others.

## Remaining gaps

- Map `props` are still not drawn by the client (only brushes).
- Weather does not blend between calendar slots; no shelter map for rain under roofs.
- Human validation of the four maps by Gabriel.
