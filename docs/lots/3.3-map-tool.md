# Lot 3.3 — `map-tool`: validator, report, top and perspective views
- Model/effort: Sonnet, medium | Depends on: 3.2 | Milestone: none

## Goal
Build `tools/map-tool` with `validate` (all rules below), a JSON report and a top-view image, plus perspective and top views, so that a faulty map is rejected with a precise reason.

## Crates
- Owns: `tools/map-tool`; the `maps validate` dispatch in `xtask` (subcommand wiring only, no change to xtask's existing interface).
- Read-only: `mapfmt`, `sim`, `render`, `core`, `assets`.
- Forbidden: all other crates. No public interface change anywhere.

## Spec (condensed from plan sections 13, 17, 1)
Command: `map-tool validate <map.ron> [--report out.json] [--image out.png]`. `cargo xtask maps validate` validates the whole catalogue (native maps in `assets/maps/`).

Rules (each violation reports rule id, map element and a precise message):
1. Schema and `format_version` correct (lot 3.1 `parse`).
2. File size at most 256 KiB (`MAX_MAP_BYTES`).
3. Triangle budget respected: whole map under 150 000 triangles (plan section 12 budget table). Value kept in a RON settings file of map-tool.
4. Each spawn stands on a floor, is outside every solid, and has 2 m of free height above it.
5. Enough spawns for the maximum player count, per team: at least 16 per team. Max players = 16 (plan section 1, "16 joueurs par partie"). The per-team reading of "nombre maximal de joueurs par équipe" is a decision.
6. Accessibility: from each spawn, every other spawn and every pickup is reachable. Plan graph = section 17 (0.5 m cells, step at most 0.45 m, practicable slope, fall, jump, jump-pad and teleporter edges). Decision: until lot 4.9 provides it, map-tool uses its own walk BFS over 0.5 m cells (capsule fits, step at most 0.45 m, slope at most 45°), without jump, drop or teleporter edges. Record this as a deviation and replace it with the `bots` graph in lot 4.9.
7. No spawn in direct line of sight of an enemy spawn within 8 m (ray cast).
8. A `KillVolume` covers the whole space under the map (every floor point below the lowest solid).
9. Balance: each team's distances to objectives and to major pickups differ by less than 5 %. Plan's pickup table (section 16) lists Soin, Munitions, Armure, Vitesse, Dégâts, Super saut, Grosse tête. Decision: major pickups = Health (Soin), Armour (Armure), Damage (Dégâts).
Informative statistics (not errors): longest line of sight, playable surface, cover density.

Report: JSON with `map_id` (BLAKE3 fingerprint), `errors[]`, `warnings[]`, `stats{}`. Image: top view PNG with spawns, pickups, zones.
Views: top view and perspective view rendered with `render` (offscreen).

## Public interfaces to create
- CLI of `map-tool` (above), JSON report schema `report_version: 1`.
- Library functions `validate(map: &MapFile) -> Report` in `map-tool` so tests call them directly.

## Acceptance criteria
- `cargo xtask test`: one fixture per rule; each faulty fixture is rejected with its own rule id; a correct fixture passes.
- `cargo xtask maps validate` on the example map: exit 0. On a fixture with a spawn inside a wall: exit non-zero, message names the spawn index and rule 4.
- `map-tool validate ... --report` writes JSON that parses and holds `map_id`.
- `cargo xtask render-test` includes the top and perspective view references for the example map.
- `cargo xtask check` passes.

## Out of scope
Navigation graph with jumps and teleporters (4.9), map editor (5.x), map statistics beyond the listed ones, network hash checks, maps catalogue content.

## Delivered (decisions and deviations)
- `tools/map-tool`: library `validate(&MapFile) -> Report` and `validate_bytes(&[u8])` (adds rules 1 and 2), CLI `validate <map.ron> [--report] [--image]` (exit 0 ok, 1 rule violations, 2 tool failure) and `view <map.ron> [--top] [--perspective]`. Report `report_version: 1`: `map_id` (BLAKE3 hex, null if unparsable), `errors[]`/`warnings[]` (`rule`, `element`, `message`), `stats{}`. Thresholds live in `tools/map-tool/settings.ron`. `cargo xtask maps validate` runs the tool on every `assets/maps/*.ron`.
- Decision (rule 5): a match has 16 players, so each team needs `ceil(max / 2)` spawns where `max = min(meta.players.1, 16)` (8 per team for a 16-player map), not 16 per team. `Any` spawns count for both teams; a map without team spawns needs `max` `Any` spawns.
- Decision (rule 4): floor within 0.1 m under the feet, standing capsule free of solids, 2 m of free height above (settings).
- Decision (rule 6): own walk search (`nav.rs`): 8 neighbours on 0.5 m cells, step and per-cell rise at most 0.45 m, capsule must fit; no jump, drop, jump-pad or teleporter edges (deviation, to replace with the `bots` graph in lot 4.9). One search from the first valid spawn (moves are symmetric). A pickup is reachable if a walkable cell lies within 1 m horizontally and 0 to 1.5 m below it.
- Decision (rule 7): enemy = other team, or any pair involving an `Any` spawn; eye height 1.6 m; ray cast between eyes.
- Decision (rule 8): the footprint of a brush is its bounding rectangle; every 0.5 m cell of it must lie under a `KillVolume` that reaches down to the lowest solid.
- Decision (rule 9): major pickups = Health and Armor (the format has no damage pickup, the plan's Damage is not representable). A team's figure is the mean walking distance from its nearest spawn to each objective, and separately to each major pickup; the two must differ by less than 5 %. Skipped with a warning when the map has neither.
- Statistics: triangles, spawn and pickup counts, playable surface (cells reached from the first spawn), longest line of sight (between spawn eyes, pickups), cover density (share of reached cells with a blocked move).
- Images: top view looks straight down, -Z at the top, rendered with `render` offscreen with spawns (disc, team colour), pickups (square) and zones (outline) drawn over it; `--image` needs a Vulkan device. References `tools/map-tool/tests/refs/example_{top,perspective}.png` are checked by `cargo xtask render-test` (bless with `--run-ignored only`).
- Edit to a file of another lot's data: `assets/testdata/example.ron` gained 4 team A spawns (6 per team after expansion, needed by rule 5 for its 12 players) and its rocket launcher pickup moved from y = 3.5 to 0.5 (it floated unreachably). The format tests still pass.
- Dependency: `map-tool` uses `faset-client::load_map` for the views (read-only use of client).
