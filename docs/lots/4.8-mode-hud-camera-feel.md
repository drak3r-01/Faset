# Lot 4.8 — Mode HUD, camera and feel
- Model/effort: Sonnet, medium | Depends on: 4.7, 2.16 | Milestone: none

## Goal
Draw the FPS HUD through the `GameModeClient::draw_hud` hook and the egui integration of lot 2.16, and implement the first-person camera and feel constants (eye height, FOV, tilt, landing dip, mouse sensitivity).

## Crates
- Owned: `client` (first-person camera, HUD binding, feel parameters), `mode-fps` (HUD data model and draw code of the FPS mode).
- Read-only: `ui` (egui integration from 2.16, theme), `render` (render-test helpers), `minigame-api` (`HudCtx`, `GameModeClient`), `sim`, `core`.
- Forbidden: public interfaces of `ui`, `render`, `minigame-api`, `sim`, `core`. A dependency that `mode-fps` would need on `ui` breaks the crate graph (mode-fps depends only on minigame-api): stop and report, or draw through `HudCtx`.

## Spec
### HUD elements
Dynamic reticle whose gap follows the current dispersion cone (pixel radius = focal_px * tan(sigma), focal_px = (H/2) / tan(fov_y/2)); health and armour; ammo (magazine / reserve); match timer; scores; kill feed; hit marker and damage numbers; speed indicator; active bonuses with remaining time; scoreboard (during RoundEnd and on Tab); class-choice screen at death and at round start.
Layout must pass the `render-test` captures at 16:9 and 16:10 (desktop sizes).

### Camera
- Eye height 1.6 m standing, 0.8 m while sliding (capsule 1.8 m, 1.0 m while sliding), transition smoothed (not instant).
- Field of view widens with speed beyond running speed (run speed 7.0 m/s). Curve and maximum are settings; the plan gives no numbers: choose and report.
- Slight roll (tilt) during lateral movement.
- Landing dip: a critically damped spring, x'' = -omega^2 (x - x_target) - 2*omega*x', kicks the eye on landing, impulse growing with fall speed.
- Weapon view spring uses the same spring form (lot 4.4 recoil).

### Sensitivity
Mouse sensitivity uses the standard 0.022 degrees per unit (yaw/pitch per raw count = 0.022 * sens). Display also in centimetres per full turn:
cm/360 = 360 * 2.54 / (0.022 * sens * dpi).

## Public interfaces to create or respect
- `client`: `FirstPersonCamera` with `update(dt, player_state) -> CameraPose` (eye position, orientation, FOV) and `FeelParams` (serde, TOML-compatible settings). Feel values are read from settings, never hard-coded.
- `mode-fps`: `HudModel` built each frame from the mode state, drawn via `draw_hud(&mut self, ui: &mut HudCtx)`.
- The render world is the only thing the renderer reads (client extracts `RenderWorld`); the camera does not touch `render`.

## Acceptance criteria
- `cargo xtask render-test` produces HUD captures matching the reference images for: standing, sliding, low health, a kill feed of three entries, the scoreboard, and the class-choice screen. Reference images are added in this lot.
- `cargo xtask test`:
  - Eye height is 1.6 m standing and 0.8 m sliding after the transition.
  - Reticle radius grows monotonically with sigma and equals focal_px * tan(sigma) within 0.5 px.
  - Sensitivity conversion: sens = 1, dpi = 800 gives cm/360 = 914.4 / 17.6 = 51.95 cm (check the printed value).
- `cargo xtask ci` is green.
