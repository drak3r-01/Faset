# Lot 6.13 - Level design (internal lot)

Not in the reference plan. The four current maps are symmetric boxes; real shooters use asymmetric, readable layouts integrated into their scenery.

- **Model/effort:** Opus, high | **Depends on:** 6.10, 6.11, 6.12 | **Branch:** `lot/6.13-level-design` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `assets/maps`, `tools/map-scripts`, `docs/map-design-guide.md` (extend).

## Goal

Three new maps with non-square shapes (L-shape, loop, lanes around a center), designed on paper first, then built on the new terrain and playtested with bots. Inspired by how big-game maps are designed and integrated into scenery, never copying an existing map (own names, layouts, landmarks).

## Spec

1. **Paper plan first** (committed in `docs/maps/<name>.md`): three lanes, contested center, flanks, multi-floor interiors, windows and sightline control, protected spawns, pickups and risk/reward spots, flow diagram, landmarks, target player count.
2. **Build** with `tools/map-scripts` on the 6.10 terrain, 6.11 vegetation and 6.12 surroundings, in the pixel style and palette.
3. **Playtest with bots** (`map-tool trial`): spawn deaths under 10 %, balanced kills per side, no dead zones, engagement distances in range; iterate and record results.
4. Old symmetric maps stay in the rotation or are retired (decision, record it).

## Acceptance

`cargo xtask ci` green (`maps validate` on all maps); trial table in the lot report; plans committed. Human validation by Gabriel pending after merge.

## Decisions (lot report)

- **Maps:** Mossgate (L-shape, village and mill pond, storm), Greyfold Quarry
  (loop around a flooded pit, overcast day), Nightjar Market (three lanes
  around a market hall, night). Plans in `docs/maps/`, scripts in
  `tools/map-scripts/map_{mossgate,greyfold,nightjar}.py`. All three are
  team maps for 8 to 16 players (8 spawns per team).
- **Symmetry:** gameplay stays symmetric for fairness (diagonal mirror x = z
  for the L, `Rotate180` for the loop, `MirrorX` for the lanes); asymmetry is
  in the outline and the landmarks. The diagonal mirror is written by the
  script (the format has no such symmetry); its blocking trees are placed by
  hand on both sides.
- **Trial targets:** spawn deaths under 10 %, kill gap between teams at most
  15 %, dead zones (4 m cells of the declared playable area that no bot
  entered) at most 25 %, mean engagement distance in the range of each plan
  (Mossgate 8 to 15 m, Greyfold 9 to 18 m, Nightjar 6 to 12 m), on seeds 1 to 3.
- **Old maps:** the four maps of lot 6.8 stay in the rotation (the catalogue
  reads `assets/maps`, which now holds seven maps). `build.py` now writes
  their 6.12 `surroundings` too, so it regenerates them byte for byte.
