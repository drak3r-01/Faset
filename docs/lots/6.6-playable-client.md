# Lot 6.6 - Playable client integration (internal lot)

Not in the reference plan. Opened after phase 6, on the model of [4.11](4.11-integration.md): the menus, the session, the HUD, the settings and the renderer each worked on their own, but the `faset` executable could not play a match (no controls, no HUD, no other players on screen, settings stored and never read).

- **Model/effort:** Opus, high | **Depends on:** 6.1-6.5, 4.11, 5.1 | **Branch:** `claude/project-thread-i3mdb8` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `client`. **Additive changes allowed in:** `mode-fps` (client view, replicated ammunition), `minigame-api` (`GameModeClient::as_any_mut`), `render` (`set_vsync`, `set_shadow_distance`).

## Goal

A player starts `faset`, hosts a game with bots (or joins one by address or from the LAN list), and plays it: mouse look, movement, shooting, a live HUD, the other players, pickups, projectiles, the weapon in hand, the pause menu, settings that take effect.

## What was done

1. **Controls** (`client::input::GameInput`): keys and mouse (named like the bindings: `KeyW`, `Mouse0`) become the tick's `InputCommand` through the user's `Bindings` and `ControlSettings`: movement axes, jump (held or once), crouch and aim (held or toggled), fire, reload, weapon slots 1/2, next/previous weapon and the wheel, scoreboard held. Mouse look uses the sensitivity (and aim sensitivity), `cm/360` constant and invert-Y of the settings. `Sprint` and `Interact` have no protocol bit yet and are not sent.
2. **Winit to egui adapter** (`client::egui_input::EguiInput`): pointer in points (scale factor x interface scale), buttons and wheel with modifiers, all letters, digits, navigation and function keys, typed text, IME commits, focus loss. It also names physical keys like the bindings do, so the rebinding screen works with modifiers and every key, and gives the executable the raw key/button/wheel stream for the game.
3. **Cursor:** grabbed (locked, else confined) and hidden while the match is on top and the window focused; released in menus, pause and on focus loss. `Escape` always opens/closes the pause menu. Raw motion comes from `DeviceEvent::MouseMotion`.
4. **Session** (`client::session`, `client::match_view`): `Session::with_registry` and `with_map_cache` give it the modes and the maps it has. It mirrors the match (`MatchMirror`), applies `MatchState` and game events, freezes the prediction outside `Warmup`/`Playing` and when dead, applies the server's speed multipliers, tallies kills and deaths, tracks projectiles and blasts, detects the local shots (magazine going down) and builds the real `HudModel` (health, armour, ammunition, weapon, speed, dispersion, timer, scoreboard, kill feed).
5. **Map transfer over UDP:** the handshake's fingerprint goes through `MapSync` (embedded map, map folder, download cache by fingerprint, else a download on channel 3); the collision world of the prediction and the map shown by the renderer come from the map the server plays. A `MapChange` of the rotation does the same and resynchronises the clock. A server that does not publish its map (no rotation) is given 4 s, then the client predicts on the default arena as before. A refused map ends the session with a message.
6. **Replicated ammunition:** `PlayView` (mode-fps) now carries the weapon index, the magazine and the reserve of the weapon in hand (5 bytes per player). `FpsClient` (new, `mode-fps::client_view`) gives the application the HUD, players and pickups behind one object-safe trait, reached through `GameModeClient::as_any_mut`.
7. **Scene** (`client::scene::MatchScene`): the other players as box characters (team colour, class build, weapon in hand, interpolated), pickups that spin and bob, projectiles (extrapolated from their creation event) and blasts, and the local weapon animated by the sway/step/recoil springs of `faset-assets`.
8. **HUD options** (`client::hud::HudOptions`): reticle style, size, thickness, gap and opacity, HUD scale, speed indicator and damage numbers from `GameSettings`. `FpsHud::new` keeps the look of lot 4.8 (the references did not change).
9. **Settings now read:** sensitivity, aim sensitivity, invert-Y, toggles and hold-jump (controls), reticle and HUD (game), weapon position, field of view and reduced shake, vsync (swapchain rebuilt live) and shadows (`Renderer::set_shadow_distance`: off, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x of the default distance). The camera is the first-person camera of 4.8 (eye height, speed FOV, roll, landing dip).

## Decisions

- **Branch:** the thread's `claude/project-thread-i3mdb8`, not a `lot/` branch (platform rule of this session).
- **Characters and weapon are boxes**, from the cubes of the loadout preview; no new mesh format.
- **Shadow quality** only changes the distance of the sun shadows (0 turns the pass off); the shadow map size is fixed.
- **Projectile and blast drawing is approximate:** no collision on the client; the explosion event removes the nearest projectile; a projectile also ends after its lifetime.
- **Kill feed and scoreboard names** are `You` and `P<id>`: the protocol carries no player names yet.
- **Test timing:** the application must be given the real time that passed (as the window does): a test that feeds a fixed 8 ms per ~12 ms frame slows the client clock and the server drops its late commands.

## Remaining gaps

- The weapon in hand is a normal instance in front of the camera: it can sink into a wall (no first-person depth pass in the renderer).
- `occlusion` and `draw_distance` settings still have no renderer hook; `Sprint` and `Interact` have no protocol bit; no chat, no gamepad.
- No sound in the match (the audio engine is started by the executable but no event plays a sound).
- Player names, class choice menu during a match, weapon pickups on the map and bot sounds are still open (see 4.11).

## Acceptance

`crates/client/tests/playable.rs` (a solo game with bots is entered through the play screen, played with `GameInput`: HUD model with weapon and ammunition, replicated players, movement, shots seen; a second client joins a hosted rotation over UDP with none of its maps and downloads the one in play), `crates/client/tests/lavapipe/match_scene.rs` (a live match drawn on lavapipe: map, bots, weapon in hand, HUD, no validation message), unit tests of `GameInput`, `EguiInput`, `MatchView`'s helpers and `HudOptions`. `FASET_MATCH_SCENE_PNG=<path>` keeps the capture of the lavapipe test.
