# Lot 8.8 - Vegetation v2 and lighting polish (phase 8, world)

Not in the reference plan. Phase 8 turns the arena maps into believable worlds (see `README.md`).

- **Model/effort:** Sonnet, high | **Depends on:** 8.2, 8.5 | **Branch:** `lot/8.8-vegetation-v2` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `assets` (vegetation recipes), `render` (grass, foliage shading, AO), `shaders`.

## Goal

Forests, meadows and shores look rich and natural.

## Spec

1. Better species: tree crowns with volume and variation, conifers, palms, reeds, flowers, moss, ferns, dead trees, per-theme sets.
2. **Ground cover** that follows the terrain material (grass only on grass), thick close-range grass with fade, wind with gusts.
3. **Lighting polish:** ambient occlusion at foliage and prop bases, soft contact shadows, per-theme colour grading; stays inside the frame budget.
4. Gameplay rule unchanged: vegetation blocks sight for bots but not bullets.

## Acceptance

`cargo xtask ci` green; lavapipe screenshots per species and per theme; dense meadow benchmark inside budget; bot LOS test unchanged.
