# Lot 4.7 — Pickups, score, free-for-all and team modes
- Model/effort: Sonnet, medium | Depends on: 4.1, 4.4 | Milestone: none

## Goal
Implement the FPS mode's pickups, score, timers and the two first modes (free-for-all and team deathmatch) on top of the `minigame-api` framework, so that a full match runs headless.

## Crates
- Owned: `mode-fps` (pickups, score rules, mode descriptors, match logic, headless test harness).
- Read-only: `minigame-api` (`GameMode`, `GameModeClient`, phases, services: teams, scoreboard, timers, bonuses), `sim`, `server`, `net` (memory transport), `protocol`, `core`.
- Forbidden: public interfaces of `minigame-api`, `sim`, `server`, `net`, `protocol`, `core`. If a service is missing from `minigame-api`, stop and report instead of adding it.

## Spec
### Match phases (server authoritative, replicated with end tick)
Warmup (waiting for players, free movement) -> Countdown 3 s -> Playing (until the timer ends or a win condition) -> RoundEnd (scoreboard 8 s) -> MapChange (next map of rotation) -> Loading -> back to Warmup.
Match timer: 300 s by default (`rotation_seconds = 300`, configurable through the mode's settings schema).

### Modes
- Free-for-all: every player for themselves.
- Team deathmatch: two teams; friendly fire off (lot 4.4 damage chain).
- Both declare a `ModeDescriptor`: id, display name, compatible map tags, bot support = yes, typed settings schema with bounds and defaults (drives the host menu, lot 6.2). Capacity: 16 players, margin to 32.

### Pickups
A pickup is a trigger sphere of radius 1 m. The server decides collection; the client waits for the server's confirmation before showing it.
| Bonus | Effect | Respawn delay |
|---|---|---|
| Heal | +50 health | 30 s |
| Ammo | reserve refilled to full | 20 s |
| Armour | absorbs 50 damage | 45 s |
| Speed | x1.3 for 10 s | 45 s |
| Damage | x1.5 for 15 s | 60 s |
| Super jump | jump speed x1.5 for 15 s | 45 s |
| Big head | enemy heads enlarged for 10 s (comic) | 60 s |
Active bonuses stack in time, each with its own timer, and feed the damage chain of lot 4.4.

### Score and elimination
Score values, the win condition and the bonus spawn rate are not given in the plan: put them in the mode settings with defaults, and report the chosen values. Death rules from lot 4.4 apply (kill feed event, respawn after 3 s).

### Mode message wire
Mode-specific messages travel as `ModeMessage(mode_id, bytes)`; `protocol` does not know the mode.

## Public interfaces to create or respect
- `impl GameMode for FpsMode` (`descriptor`, `on_match_start`, `on_player_join`, `on_player_leave`, `on_tick`, `on_event`, `result`, `replicated_state`) and `impl GameModeClient` (`apply_state`, `on_event`, `draw_hud`, the HUD body belongs to lot 4.8).
- Register statically: `registry.register::<FpsMode>()`. No dynamic loading.
- Two descriptors: `fps.ffa` and `fps.team` (ids chosen by the session, documented).

## Acceptance criteria
- `cargo xtask test`:
  - `full_match_headless_ffa` and `full_match_headless_team`: a server with a memory transport and N headless clients (no window, no GPU) completes Warmup, Countdown, Playing, RoundEnd, and reports a result.
  - Pickup: collection is decided by the server; a client without confirmation does not show the pickup as taken.
  - Each bonus effect and its duration (10, 15 s) and return delay (20 to 60 s) is checked.
  - Team friendly fire is off; FFA allows damage between all players.
- `cargo xtask ci` is green.

## Out of scope
Shot mechanics (4.4), projectiles (4.5), class data (4.6), HUD, camera and feel (4.8), bots (4.9, 4.10), map rotation and catalogue (phase 5), host menus (6.2).
