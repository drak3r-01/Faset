# Lot 8.11 - Rebuild Red Mesa, Greyfold Quarry and Nightjar Market (phase 8, world)

Not in the reference plan. Phase 8 turns the arena maps into believable worlds (see `README.md`).

- **Model/effort:** Opus, high | **Depends on:** 8.4, 8.6, 8.7, 8.8 | **Branch:** `lot/8.11-maps-canyon-quarry-market` (one PR)
- **Owns:** `assets/maps`, `tools/map-scripts`, `docs/maps/`, `docs/map-design-guide.md`.

## Goal

Rebuild Red Mesa, Greyfold Quarry, Nightjar Market as places that feel real: a desert canyon, a flooded quarry and a night market.

## Spec

1. **Terrain first:** generate then sculpt a 1 to 2 km landscape (lot 8.3), pick the 150 to 300 m playable zone inside it and let natural boundaries close it (lot 8.4).
2. **Paper plan updated** in `docs/maps/<name>.md` (lanes, sightlines, spawns, pickups) so the gameplay stays readable; the old layout is kept where it played well.
3. **Dress the world:** landmarks visible from the zone (8.2), real props (8.6, 8.7), vegetation (8.8); no leftover block-looking props.
4. **Playtest with bots** (`map-tool trial`) with the lot 6.13 targets (spawn deaths under 10 %, kill gap at most 15 %, dead zones at most 25 %), record the trial table.
5. Keep map transfer size and load time inside the limits measured in 8.1.
